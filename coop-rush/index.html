Privacy Policy – Coop Rush

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Coop Rush (“the Game”) is developed by BearMakingGames, based in France (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is used when you play the Game, why, and the choices you have. If you have any question, contact us at BearMakingGame.Contact@protonmail.com.

1. In short

  • We do not run accounts or servers: we do not receive or store any personal information about you.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. Our advertising partners may collect device information to show those ads.
  • Ads are personalized only if you are 16 or older and you agree to it. You can change your choice at any time with the settings button (gear) in the main menu.
  • Players under 16 never see personalized ads.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves on your device: your progress, eggs and hats, game settings, the year of birth you enter on first launch, and your privacy choices. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (operated by Unity Technologies and its affiliate ironSource Mobile) and the ad networks it works with, including Google AdMob. To deliver ads, these partners may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (on Android: the Google advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country, region);
  • technical information about your device: model, operating system, language, network type;
  • information about the Game and about the ads you see or tap.

They use this information to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. If you have agreed to it, they may also use it to show ads that match your interests (personalized ads).

Each partner processes this information under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity / LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services

4. Your choices

  • Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland): if you are 16 or older, the Game asks whether our partners may use your data for personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the settings button (gear) in the main menu.
  • Do not sell or share my data (United States): the same settings screen lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information for advertising.
  • Device settings: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google › Ads).

5. Children

The Game is designed for a general audience that may include children. On first launch, the Game asks for your year of birth, without suggesting any answer. This year is stored only on your device.

  • Players under 13: our ad partners are told to treat the player as a child (under the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA). Only non-personalized ads are shown and no personal information is knowingly collected for advertising.
  • Players under 16: only non-personalized ads are shown, and no consent is asked.

If you are a parent and believe your child’s information was collected, contact us at BearMakingGame.Contact@protonmail.com and we will help you reach the relevant partner.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Personalized advertising relies on your consent. Other advertising processing (showing non-personalized ads, measurement, fraud prevention) relies on our partners’ legitimate interests or on your consent, as described in their policies.

7. Purchases

The Game offers an in-app purchase that removes ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play: we do not receive your payment information. Your purchase is recorded by Google Play, so it can be restored if you reinstall the Game, and a copy of its status is stored on your device.

8. International transfers and retention

Our partners may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards required by law (such as standard contractual clauses). They keep information for the time described in their own policies. Information stored on your device is kept until you uninstall the Game.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to its use, to withdraw your consent and to complain to a data protection authority (in France: the CNIL, cnil.fr). Since we do not hold your information ourselves, requests about advertising data are handled by our partners; contact us and we will help you.

10. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the Game adds new features. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Contact
